How would one describe a businessman? A businessman is traditionally defined as an individual who orchestrates the exchange of goods, services, or capital to generate profit and maintain a sustainable enterprise. Always clad in a fitted suit, leather shoes, not a hair out of place, a watch that costs more than my life, and a prim expression. To an outsider, these businessmen look soulless, as if the only routine they follow is as follows: work, eat, sleep, repeat. To the external observer, this archetype often appears "soulless," a byproduct of a recursive behavioral loop: work, consumption, and rest. While this hyper-regimented lifestyle is designed to manufacture a facade of financial stability, the contemporary global landscape remains inherently volatile. In this context, the businessman’s perceived security is a fragile construct, predicated on the maintenance of a highly controlled, artificial environment.
The intersection of environmental effect and user experience provides a fertile ground for exploring character evolution. By extracting this "prim and proper" agent from his sterile, predictable habitat (of an office space) and transplanting him into a non-traditional setting, we disrupt his habitual interactions. The proposed environment is a dense, hyper-saturated fog-scape. A liminal space populated by miles of contorted, arboreal growths that mirror human anatomy more than traditional foliage which creates a landscape of visual and psychological claustrophobia.The user’s engagement with this character is defined by a series of high-stakes bifurcations. The goal is to move the character from a state of passive systemic participation to active, risky engagement. This is achieved through several core interaction loops: Choosing between divergent paths in an environment forcing a reliance on instinct, interactions where the user must engage in physical or tactical maneuvers that threaten his physical integrity and navigating interpersonal relationships with other displaced entities. The user is granted the agency to either cultivate deep, communal bonds or terminate those connections. 
The proposed narrative framework serves as a examination of character deconstruction through environmental displacement. By isolating an agent defined by systemic rigidity and transplanting him into an environment that he isn’t used to, the project creates a crucible for adaptation.
